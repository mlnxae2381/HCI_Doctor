About HCI DocTor

Service Overview

HCI DocTor is an AI-powered medical diagnosis assistance system built on Google's HAI-DEF (Health AI Developer Foundations) models. It analyzes medical imaging and acoustic data to provide early-stage health screening.

Technologies

  • MedGemma 1.5-4b-it - CT and X-ray image analysis
  • HeAR (Health Acoustic Representations) - Cough and breathing sound analysis
  • LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) - Efficient model fine-tuning

Disclaimer

This service is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis and should be used for reference purposes only. Always consult a licensed medical professional for accurate diagnosis.
